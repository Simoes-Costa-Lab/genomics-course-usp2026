#  Research Project Guidelines

This page contains the guidelines for the Research Project.

---

# Overview of Research Projects Structure

## Research Projects will be presented on Friday (7/3) by 6 groups of 5 students

- Presentations will be 30mins + 10mins of Q&A;
- Projects will be evaluated as a grant application;
- Any topic is OK as long as it is centered in genomics and uses techniques discussed in class.

---

# Structure of the Specific Aims

## Title / big question
- One slide. What is the biological problem?

## Background and significance
- 3–5 slides. What is known? Why does it matter?

## Knowledge gap
- 1 slide. What do we still not understand?

## Central hypothesis / overall objective
- 1 slide. What do you want to accomplish

## Specific Aims
- Ideally 2-3 aims, 2-3 slides per aim. For each aim:
- What question does this aim answer?
- What techniques will be used?
- What are the key controls?
- What result would support or refute the hypothesis?
- What are possible pitfalls/alternatives?

## Deliverables and impact
- 1–2 slides. What will the field know after this project? Why is it important?
